🧠 Catch-Up Without Getting Stuck

Use this whenever a prerequisite is blocking the next concept.

1. Identify What exactly is confusing?

2. Find the Gap What prerequisite is missing?
→
3. Learn 20–30 min targeted review
→
4. Test Explain or solve without help
→
5. Move Basic mastery = move on
Important: You are not trying to completely master the old topic before continuing. You are trying to reach minimum viable understanding: enough understanding to successfully engage with the next concept.
What counts as "enough"?

You should be able to explain the basic idea in your own words, identify when it applies, and solve a basic representative problem without copying the solution.

What if I still can't do it after 30 minutes?

Don't spend the entire evening fighting one concept. Break it into a smaller prerequisite. Ask: "What would I need to know to understand THIS?" Work one level lower, then retry.

What if I can do basic problems but advanced ones are hard?

Move on. Put the topic in your later-review queue. Advanced mastery can come through spaced and interleaved practice.

📚 The Study System

Use different techniques for different jobs.

RETRIEVAL

Don't just reread

Close the notes and force yourself to retrieve the information. For technical courses, this means solving problems without seeing the solution first.

SPACING

Return later

Do not study something once and abandon it. Revisit it later, especially after you think you've learned it.

Example: Learn Monday → recall Tuesday → practice Thursday → mixed problems Sunday.
INTERLEAVING

Mix problems

Once you understand the individual topics, mix them. This forces you to decide which method applies.

Instead of 15 identical questions:
A → B → C → A → C → B
TEACH

Explain it simply

Pick a concept and explain it as if you're teaching someone who has never seen it. Wherever your explanation breaks down, you've found a knowledge gap.

💡 Quick Rules

I'm procrastinating. What do I do?

Don't negotiate with yourself. Set a 10-minute timer and start one problem. You only need to commit to beginning.

I don't understand something.

Don't blindly move on and don't spend the whole day stuck. Use Catch-Up Mode: identify → prerequisite → targeted learning → test → move.

I got a bad grade.

Don't spiral. Determine what caused the lost marks: knowledge gap, careless mistake, misunderstanding, time management, or insufficient practice. Then change your preparation.

I had a terrible day.

The next action matters more than the guilt. Start the next planned block. Never turn one bad day into two.

I have no motivation.

Motivation isn't required. The schedule decides what happens. Your job is to begin the block.
